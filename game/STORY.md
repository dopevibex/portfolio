# SANCTUM: THE BOUND SOVEREIGN
*A Mini God-of-War-Inspired Action Game*

---

## 1. PREMISE & LORE
You are **Vaelor, The Bound Sovereign**. Once the iron monarch of the Obsidian Reach, you were betrayed by the High Inquisitors, stripped of your crown, and bound in heavy soul-chains. Cast into the abyssal Sanctum—a submerged, crumbling gothic cathedral suspended in the void—you awaken. In your hand remains only the **Cinder Cleaver**, a brutal notched blade wrapped in the crimson chains that once held you captive.

To reclaim your name and crown, you must fight through the cathedral's consecrated ruins and face the keeper of the seal: **Malphas, The Throne Warden**.

---

## 2. NARRATOR & SCRIPT STRUCTURE
*Narrator: The Ashen Chronicler (Low, resonant, restrained, ancient).*

### Act I: Prologue (The Awakening)
*Scene: Cold darkness. Distant iron chains clinking. A low, heavy drone of wind.*
- **Subtitle 1 (Prologue):** *"They broke your crown upon the altar stone, yet left the iron in your blood."*
- **Subtitle 2 (Prologue):** *"Rise, Sovereign. The cathedral bell tolls for what remains of you."*

### Act II: Encounter 1 (The Ashen Thralls)
*Scene: Dust falls from ruined gothic rib-vaults. Shambling husks with rusted blades step out of the shadows.*
- **Subtitle 3 (Thralls):** *"The forgotten dead still wear your sigil. Release them from their vigil."*
- **Subtitle 4 (Thralls Defeated):** *"Their ashes settle. But deeper iron stirs ahead."*

### Act III: Encounter 2 (The Shielded Brute)
*Scene: A towering armored penitent carrying a slab of reinforced gravestone steps into the light.*
- **Subtitle 5 (Brute):** *"The Bastion of Malphas yields to neither prayers nor blunt blows. Time your parry, or be crushed."*
- **Subtitle 6 (Brute Execution):** *"Brute iron yields to unbroken will."*

### Act IV: Encounter 3 (The Void Weaver)
*Scene: A hovering wraith cloaked in charred vestments floats above the broken pillars, channeling crimson pyres.*
- **Subtitle 7 (Caster):** *"A coward's magic seeks the high rafters. Cast the Cinder Cleaver and drag it down."*
- **Subtitle 8 (Caster Slain):** *"The air burns cold. The gates of the High Dais groan open."*

### Act V: The Boss — Malphas, The Throne Warden
*Scene: High Dais. Monolithic gargoyles. Malphas drops from the broken spire, dual executioner axes shattering the stone.*
- **Subtitle 9 (Boss Phase 1):** *"Malphas... the warden who hammered the chains to your flesh."*
- **Subtitle 10 (Boss Phase 2 - At 50% HP):** *"His pride breaks! The pyre ignites—unleash the Sovereign's Wrath!"*
- **Subtitle 11 (Boss Defeated):** *"The Warden falls. The chain is shattered. The Sanctum belongs to the Sovereign once more."*

### Act VI: Epilogue
*Scene: The camera pulls out slowly over the fallen Warden. Vaelor pulls the Cinder Cleaver back into his palm. The distant dawn glints across the black horizon.*
- **Subtitle 12 (Ending):** *"No crown of gold awaits. But your name echoes through the deep."*
